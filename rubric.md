# Mathematics AI Response Evaluation Rubric

Score each criterion from **1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)**. Apply the same standard to every response and cite concrete evidence.

| Criterion | Weight | What to check |
|---|---:|---|
| Mathematical correctness | 40% | Correct calculations, formulas, final answer and units |
| Reasoning and validity | 25% | Each step follows logically and uses valid rules |
| Instruction adherence | 15% | Answers the prompt and follows requested constraints |
| Clarity and communication | 10% | Explanation is understandable and well organised |
| Completeness | 10% | Required steps, assumptions, units or verification are included |

## Score interpretation
- **5:** Fully meets the criterion; no meaningful issue.
- **4:** Strong; only a minor issue.
- **3:** Partly meets the criterion; a noticeable weakness.
- **2:** Major weakness or error.
- **1:** Fails the criterion.

## Evaluator checklist
1. Restate the task and identify constraints.
2. Independently solve or verify the problem.
3. Check every material reasoning step.
4. Identify the first incorrect step, if any.
5. Score each criterion and explain the evidence.
6. Distinguish mathematical errors from style preferences.

A weighted score is a structured summary, not a substitute for expert judgment. A serious mathematical error may warrant rejection even if the writing is clear.
